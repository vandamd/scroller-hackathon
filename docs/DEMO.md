# Demo: 90 seconds maximum

Aim for 75–85 seconds, leaving room for playback differences.

- 0–10s: “Scroller lets a little office do the scrolling. You get a finite stack
  instead of an endless feed.” Show the office and a card immediately.
- 10–25s: Show Google sign-in and X connection briefly. Never record typing an
  auth_token, developer tools, environment files or the account's personal history.
  Use an authorised demonstration account. Mention Instagram is a stretch.
- 25–40s: Show the real scan and taste observations. Explain that they describe
  valuable kinds of posts, discovered from saved history and some still images.
  Cut the waiting time; do not suggest the scan took less time than it did.
- 40–65s: Read one post, open media, Like, Skip and Dislike. Show Tanner's
  reactions and the completed stack. Like queues a real X like; Skip is neutral.
- 65–75s: Explain that Convex prepares new posts in the background while keeping
  the current stack stable, and Luna makes bounded concurrent content judgements.
- 75–85s: “Codex helped build the UI, backend and worker in parallel, checked real
  APIs, and traced a stuck login to the X library.” Show the public repo link.

Before sharing, play the exported video and confirm duration, readable text and
no passwords, tokens, private history, notifications or account details. Open the
repository, video and hosted demo in a signed-out browser and confirm each is
publicly accessible. The Paper document is supporting material and may need
separate sharing; it is not a substitute for the working video.

The current connection uses auth_token, not email/password. Describe this
explicitly; a private session demo must not imply a public OAuth integration.
